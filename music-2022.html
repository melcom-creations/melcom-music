🔊 Track Information

Actually, I had planned to completely rework my track Fernweh from 2018. The Mod file is not very well composed. However, in the middle of the work, I changed my mind and worked on a second part. Many of the samples were created by myself. The drum set was specifically created for this track.

You can consider this version as an extended version. I created a special 8-bit version for ModArchive.org and uploaded it there.

🪐 One Story, Three Tracks: The Fernweh Saga
A TimeCop follows an ancient distress signal into the Orion Nebula... He lands back on his own Earth, only to find it a dead world. The only thing left to greet him is a single, flickering sign: WELCOME
