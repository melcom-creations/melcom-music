🚀 melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v3.0.0 (Halloween Edition)

📅 Saturday, October 25, 2025

Software

A Complete Rewrite with Batch Processing and a New UI

I'm thrilled to announce the official release of the FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v3.0.0 (Halloween Edition)! 🎃 This version is not just an update; it's a complete rewrite from the ground up, designed to be more powerful, stable, and user-friendly.

The biggest highlights of this major overhaul include:

  • Batch Processing: The most requested feature is here! Add multiple files or entire folders and normalize them all in one go.
  • Integrated Audio Player: A new mini-player lets you preview tracks with full playlist controls before you process them.
  • Modern, Redesigned UI: The interface has been completely redesigned for a more intuitive and efficient workflow.
  • Increased Stability: Thanks to a new threading model, the application is now more robust and responsive than ever.

🚀 melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v2.2.2 Released!

📅 Friday, October 3, 2025

Software

Featuring Audio Previews and a Real-Time Process Log

I'm happy to announce a significant update to my FFmpeg Audio Normalizer. Version 2.2.2 introduces two powerful features designed to improve your workflow and provide much better feedback during the normalization process.

  • New Audio Preview: You can now use the new "Play" and "Stop" buttons to instantly listen to your selected audio file before processing. This helps ensure you've loaded the correct track without leaving the application.
  • Live Process Information: The process window has been completely overhauled. It now shows a real-time log of FFmpeg's output, so you can see exactly what's happening at every step. A scrollbar has also been added for convenience.

► melcom's chiptune archive now on scene.org

📅 Monday, June 30, 2025

Archive

Almost 20 years ago, some demosceners and I got together with the idea to build a chiptune archive. We received incredible support from the community, especially from our IRC channel #coders.ger and the website back2roots.org. As more tracks were sent in, the archive grew, but it was discontinued shortly after I left the demoscene in 2004.

Only one snapshot of this archive from February 5, 2005, survived. It was backed up and eventually made its way to archive.org. Today, I'm thrilled to announce it has found a new, permanent home on scene.org!

melcom's ChipTune Archive (2005-02-05) contains:

  • Over 2,000 classic adlib modules
  • Over 4,200 classic chiptune modules
  • Over 1,700 classic ym modules

I recommend XMPlay for listening, but you will need various plugins to play all the modules. If you have trouble finding them, just write to me, and I'll help you out.

A huge thank you to menace for making this possible by hosting the archive on Scene.org. Thanks, man!
