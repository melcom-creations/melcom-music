melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v4.1.2 (Herbstklang Edition)

Thursday, October 22, 2026

Software

Clearer results, a flexible workspace and a warm autumn theme

The Herbstklang Edition is here! Version 4.1.2 makes it easier to analyze your original audio, check your normalized files, export reports, and arrange the workspace to suit your needs.

Highlights of the new release:

  • Batch Analysis Results: Review loudness, True Peak, LRA, PLR and signal details for your original files, with comparisons against the selected targets.
  • Optional Output Verification and Results: Check the finished file's loudness and True Peak, then compare original measurements, targets and encoded output in the new Results window.
  • Reports and CSV Export: Copy individual or complete reports and export analysis and normalization results to CSV. Both result windows open automatically after completed runs.
  • Adjustable Workspace: Resize the file list, settings area and process log with draggable dividers. Window size and divider positions are saved, with a corner resize grip and a reset option available.
  • Encoder Headroom: Add 0 to 3 dB of headroom before encoding MP3, M4A or OGG files.
  • More Comfortable Audio Work: Peak-to-Loudness Ratio (PLR) is now included in the statistics, and the tool remembers your audio and artwork folders between sessions.
  • Metadata and Artwork Fixes: Improved MP3 URL compatibility with players such as XMPlay, preserved tags during artwork changes, and corrected PNG and JPEG cover handling for M4A files.

The new herbstklang theme brings warm brown backgrounds, amber accents and cream-colored text. It is the default when no theme has been saved. The release also includes fixes for normalization accuracy, silent files and audio format handling.

Take a look at the updated screenshots, full changelog and downloads on the project page. I hope you enjoy the Herbstklang Edition!

melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v4.1.1 (Flickwerk Edition)

Tuesday, July 14, 2026

Software

Careful repairs, clearer options and optional update notifications

The Flickwerk Edition is here! Version 4.1.1 brings together a wide range of careful repairs and practical improvements across configuration handling, FFmpeg processing, metadata preservation, themes, documentation and everyday usability.

Highlights of the new v4.1.1 release:

  • Optional Update Notifications with automatic startup checks, manual checks and optional pre-release detection.
  • Redesigned Options Window with a clearer layout for appearance, FFmpeg, logging and update settings.

Flickwerk Edition focuses on stability, clarity and the small details that make everyday audio work more reliable.

melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v3.1.0 (Feierabend Edition)

Friday, February 20, 2026

Software

Professional Two-Pass Normalization and Quality Preservation

I'm excited to announce the release of FFmpeg Audio Normalizer v3.1.0 (Feierabend Edition)! This version is a major milestone, introducing professional-grade features that make the tool even more versatile for musicians and audiophiles.

Key highlights of this update:

  • Linear Two-Pass Normalization: A high-precision mode that analyzes audio first to calculate a perfect gain adjustment while keeping the original dynamic range (LRA) intact.
  • Quality Preservation: WAV and FLAC outputs now automatically preserve the source's original sample rate and bit depth (e.g., 24-bit/96kHz).
  • Metadata Preservation: All tags (Artist, Title, Album) are now correctly mapped and transferred to the output file.
  • Automated Cleanup: The program now ensures that temporary work files are removed even if a process is cancelled.

Reflecting on 2025: A Year of Building, Creating, and Looking Ahead

Wednesday, December 31, 2025

Personal

Hey everyone,

As 2025 comes to a close, I wanted to look back on what has been a huge year of learning and growth. I dove into different programming languages and created tools like melcom's FFmpeg Audio Normalizer, which I hope has made life easier for fellow creators managing audio for platforms like YouTube and Spotify.

On the music front, I spent a lot of time learning FL Studio, but I remain faithful to OpenMPT for building my own unique samples and instruments with VST plugins. I also contributed to a major collaborative project set to be released in 2026, featuring one (or maybe even two) massive tracks from me-we'll see how the runtimes turn out!

A Personal Wish for 2026

I would love for my new tracks, as well as my old ones (and the even older mod files), to get more attention and downloads. I know that my tracks don't really-or rather, less and less-sound like the typical demoscene style. But then again, hasn't that always been the case for me?

Anyway, I would just be happy to see more downloads. I promote my work enough, and I'm also active on platforms like Bsky, where you're always welcome to visit me. Feedback? I often ask myself, what is that? I simply get no feedback, no matter how good a track might be. Even some of the big musicians from the demoscene are stumped; they're enthusiastic about my work themselves but don't understand the lack of engagement either and are out of ideas on how to help.

So, those are the "little things" I'm simply wishing for more of in 2026.

Have a great start to the new year, and take care.

All the best,
melcom
