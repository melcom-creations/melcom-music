This is my contribution to The Sound of SceneSat Vol. 7!

"Rotten" personifies a unique species that resides on a distant planet, spreading suffering and pain. This ominous species torments other beings on the planet. I envision this planet as an eternal realm of suffering. "Rotten" embodies the absolute evil, ceaselessly consuming the essence of others and inflicting boundless torment upon them.
