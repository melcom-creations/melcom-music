  • Arkos Tracker NEW Cross-platform chiptune tracker for AY-3-8912 and YM2149 sound chips.
  • BeRoTracker Impulse Tracker-style music tracker with sample editing, plugins and MIDI.
  • ChibiTracker Cross-platform music tracker inspired by Impulse Tracker.
  • DefleMask Chiptune tracker supporting multiple classic sound chips and game systems.
  • enotracker Music tracker for the Atari POKEY sound chip.
  • FamiTracker Windows music tracker for NES and Famicom sound chips.
  • FastTracker II clone Portable recreation of the DOS classic FastTracker II.
  • Game Boy Tracker Project history of the desktop tracker for Game Boy music.
  • GoatTracker 2 C64 SID music tracker with desktop editing tools.
  • HivelyTracker AHX and HVL chip music tracker with up to 16 channels.
  • MadTracker Windows music tracker with VST plugins and ReWire support.
  • MilkyTracker Open-source music tracker focused on FastTracker II-compatible modules.
  • OpenMPT 💙 Pick Windows tracker supporting many module formats, plugins and MIDI.
  • PreTracker NEW Windows editor for creating Amiga chip music and synthesized instruments.
  • ProTracker 2 clone Portable recreation of the Amiga ProTracker 2 workflow.
  • Schism Tracker 💙 Pick Open-source, cross-platform recreation of Impulse Tracker.
  • SID Factory II Cross-platform editor for Commodore 64 SID music.
  • SID-Wizard Native C64 music tracker with MIDI and XM conversion tools.
  • Skale Tracker FastTracker-style Windows tracker with VST and SoundFont support.
  • TIATracker Music tracker for the Atari 2600 TIA sound chip.
  • Trackerboy Desktop music tracker for the Game Boy sound hardware.
  • TrackMeister 💙 Pick Fullscreen module player for tracked music competitions.
  • VGM Music Maker Shiru's Sega Mega Drive, Master System and Game Gear tracker; original release thread.
